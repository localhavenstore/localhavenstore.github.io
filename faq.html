FAQ

What do I need?

A small always-on computer (chapter 2 helps you choose - a mini PC with 32-64 GB of memory is ideal), a USB microphone and a speaker for voice, and a phone with a chat app for remote control. No programming.

Do I need a big graphics card?

No. The course is built and tested on a mini PC with integrated graphics and plenty of shared memory.

Does anything go to the cloud?

The language model, speech-to-text, wake word and voice all run on your hardware. The only outside connections are the ones you choose (for example the chat app on your phone), and chapter 11 shows how to keep them safe.

Is this someone's personal assistant for sale?

No. You build your own. The course is based on a real system, but everything is rewritten as general, tested steps; nobody gets access to your system and you never install someone else's.

How does the membership work?

EUR 8 a month or EUR 64 a year through Gumroad (EU VAT is added at checkout where it applies), cancel any time from your Gumroad library. You get all chapters, all templates and every new chapter while you are a member. Gumroad handles payment, receipts and VAT.

Refunds?

30-day money-back guarantee - just reply to your receipt e-mail.

Is there live support or a community?

No - that keeps the price low. Instead there is this FAQ, updated from members' questions. You can always write to localhavenstore@gmail.com.

Was this made with AI?

Yes, with AI assistance: chapters are drafted with AI tools from real build notes, then tested and checked before they are published.

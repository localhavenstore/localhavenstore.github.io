Localhaven privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026. Contact: localhavenstore@gmail.com.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no forms. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data (such as IP addresses) as described in its own privacy statement.

Purchases

Payments happen on Gumroad or Etsy, never on this site. They are the sellers of record and handle your payment data under their own privacy policies. We receive what those platforms share with every seller (for example your order, e-mail address and country) and use it only to deliver the product, send product updates and answer questions.

Localhaven publisher (our Pinterest app)

"Localhaven publisher" is a private app that Localhaven uses only for its own Pinterest account. It is not offered to other people and never signs in anyone else.

  • What it does: creates Pins about our own products on our own boards, and reads our own boards, Pins and Pin statistics. Every Pin is approved by a person, one by one, before it is posted.
  • What it does not do: it does not read, store or contact other Pinterest users, does not comment, follow, message or save other people's Pins, and does not run ads.
  • Data it keeps: the access token for our own account (stored encrypted on our own server) and a log of the Pins we posted. No data about other people. We do not sell or share any data.
  • You can see everything it posts on our public Pinterest profile.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time: localhavenstore@gmail.com.

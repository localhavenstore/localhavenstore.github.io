How a ticket is handled

  1. You report it

    By e-mail, the portal or phone. You get a ticket number straight away.

  2. A named engineer answers

    Within the response time of your plan - a person who knows your office, not a call centre.

  3. We fix it, remotely if we can

    Most problems are solved without a visit. You see every step in the ticket.

  4. You confirm it works

    The ticket closes only when you say so. Once a month you get a short report.

Questions we hear first

Do we need to replace our computers?

No. We start with what you have and tell you which devices are too old for security updates - with a date, not a sales pitch.

What happens to our data if we leave?

It is yours. We hand over every password, the backup and a written description of your setup within five working days.

Can you take over from our current IT person?

Yes. We do a one-week handover: we list every account, device and subscription, then change the admin passwords together with you.

Do you support all kinds of computers?

Yes - laptops and desktops of any make, phones and tablets, and the common office e-mail and document suites.

How fast can we start?

Usually within two weeks: one visit to see your office, then we set up monitoring and backups.

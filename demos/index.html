Website demos

Three websites for fictional small businesses, each built from scratch with its own design. They are fast (Lighthouse 99-100), work on phones, and need no server: a folder of files that any free static host can serve. Nothing on them can be bought or booked.

  • Oriel Street Bakehouse

    A bakery whose site shows what is coming out of the oven next, today's board, hours and cake orders.

  • Ampere & Pipe Co.

    An electrician and plumber: call now, prices, service area and a three-step quote request.

  • Quayside Desk

    A small IT support company: a support ticket being solved, a plan comparison and FAQ.
